# 📉 Telecom Customer Churn Analysis

This project analyzes customer data from a telecom company to understand the key factors influencing customer churn — the rate at which customers stop using the company's service.

## 📁 Dataset

- Total Customers: 7,043  
- Churned Customers: 1,869  
- Churn Rate: 26.54%

## 📊 Objective

The main objective is to identify:
- Why are customers leaving (churning)?
- What patterns or behaviors are common among churned customers?
- How can the company reduce churn and improve customer retention?

---

## 📌 Key Insights

- **Tenure**: Customers with only 1–2 months of usage had the highest churn. Long-term users are more loyal.
- **Contract Type**: Month-to-month customers churned the most. 1 or 2-year contract customers were more stable.
- **Senior Citizens**: Made up 16% of users but had a higher churn rate.
- **Services Used**: Churn was higher when Online Backup, Tech Support, or Streaming TV were not used.
- **Payment Method**: Electronic Check users had the highest churn rate. Credit Card and Bank Transfer users were more stable.

---

## ✅ Conclusion

To reduce churn, the telecom company should:
- Encourage long-term contracts (1 or 2 years)
- Bundle essential services (Online Security, Tech Support)
- Reduce reliance on electronic check payments
- Focus retention efforts on new users and senior citizens
